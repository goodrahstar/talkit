What it can't do yet.

I'd rather you hear it from me than find out on the train.

It opens once per session.

iOS only lets an app start the microphone while it's on screen. So the first time, Talkit opens for a second and you tap back. After that it stays ready for up to an hour.

You'll see the orange dot.

That's iOS showing the mic is ready. Talkit only keeps audio between the moment you press and the moment you let go.

You install it from a Mac, for now.

No App Store or TestFlight yet. You build it with Xcode from the GitHub repo, and a free Apple account renews every 7 days.

Why I built it.

notes

On my Mac I dictate everything. I hold a key, talk, let go, and the text is there.

On my phone, every app I tried made me wait while the model loaded, or made me copy and paste between apps. By then I'd have been faster typing.

So I built the version I wanted: a keyboard you hold to talk, with the model already awake on the phone.
